# Contributing

Read the [Documentation Guide](docs/README.md), active [Project Status](docs/states/CURRENT.md), [Execution Rules](docs/rules/EXECUTION.md), and [Task Reading Set](docs/README.md#task-reading-set) before changing work.

## Change Submission

Follow the applicable [Architecture Rules](docs/rules/ARCHITECTURE.md), [Coding Rules](docs/rules/CODING.md), and source/research policy. Research references and locally supplied ROM material remain non-product inputs unless an admitted task says otherwise.

## Review Record

Record the ownership boundary, logic or user-visible effect, provenance and redistributability impact, verification evidence, and any deferred issue or owner-approved exception.

## Commits And Tracking

Use the M/T/S/P lifecycle in [Execution Rules](docs/rules/EXECUTION.md). A commit records a complete reviewed P; a local ROM-derived binary is never release evidence or a committed artifact.
