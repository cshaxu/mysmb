# Text Object And State Visual Audit

Owner-requested queue-head candidate. Not admitted;no numeric T or S is
allocated. CURRENT remains the sole active packet;existing T24 execution is
not interrupted by this queue edit.

## Objective And Boundary

Audit the current text shapes and colors of every character,object and its
supported states. Publish the complete labeled text-state gallery for owner
visual review before deciding which designs to change. Do not infer quality
from historical test passes or require every design to be replaced.

This is a shared text-presentation task. Original game logic,graphical output,
input,physics and platform artwork remain unchanged. Preserve T23 retainer
repairs. No bitmap-to-character sampling,selector re-execution or new gameplay
state is permitted. This work earns no ROM-node/control certification credit.

## Finite Coverage Inventory

Build a versioned manifest from current source selectors,committed observations,
authored templates and background classification. Historical T17's17observer
families,46authored kinds and101background positions are starting references,
not proof that every current combination is present or visually satisfactory.
Publish the actual supported/reachable combinations and missing cases by name;
do not blindly multiply every kind by every pose or palette slot.

Include:

- Mario/Luigi small,large,fire and invincibility palette phases:standing,
  running phases,jump,skid,crouch,throw,death,swimming/kicking and climbing.
- Growth/1UP mushrooms,flower,star and emergence/partial visibility;coins,
  question/spent/hidden/bouncing/broken blocks and color-animation phases.
- Every supported enemy identity and appearance:Goomba,red/green/dark turtles,
  winged turtles,shell movement/defeat/revival,inversion,Beetle,Bloober,fish,
  flying fish,Bullet Bill,Piranha,Hammer Bro,Lakitu,Spiny/egg,Podoboo and Bowser
  components/phases;Princess and Toad.
- Fireballs,explosions,firebars,hammer phases,chunks,vines,platforms,springs,
  flags,bubbles,jump coins,floating scores and all other observed components.
- Terrain/scenery forms:ground,stairs,bricks,pipes in each orientation,clouds,
  hills,bushes,trees/fences,water/coral,castle,ropes/pulleys/chains,cannons,axe
  and flags. Include connected spans and foreground/background distinctions.
- Information text and scene overlays:HUD,title,intermediate lives,game-over,
  victory/ending and warp messages;all supported dynamic score values.

Each manifest row identifies family/object,source-selected state or graphics,
template/pose,facing,inversion,committed palette and ink roles,scene background,
visibility condition,renderer owner and evidence path. Distinguish native
observed fixtures from controlled descriptor fixtures. List unsupported,
missing and intentionally identical variants explicitly. Register any scope
amendment with its reason and added/removed rows rather than silently growing
the checklist.

## Proposed Delivery Chains

Assign actual S identifiers and component/size estimates only at admission.

1. Inventory and gallery:enumerate the finite manifest,render current shared
   authored text cells,and verify gallery completeness against it. No product
   artwork repair before the owner can inspect the baseline.
2. Visual decisions:record a per-row verdict of keep,shape improvement,color
   improvement,missing/incorrect state or awaiting owner review. Give a concrete
   reason and proposal for every suggested change. Review object groups with
   the owner one by one;retain undecided rows as pending rather than approved.
3. Approved repair cohorts:group related objects/templates and shared palette
   consumers into bounded S scopes. Show before/after gallery entries and
   representative scene placement,repair/re-audit within that S,then continue.

Do not allocate one implementation S per trivial glyph or import unfinished
ROM-certification work into this presentation audit.

## Gallery And Review Contract

- Deliver a complete overview/contact sheet plus readable per-family pages,
  with stable case IDs and labels for every enumerated state/color variant.
  Large sheets must also have enlarged views;do not shrink characters until
  their details are unreadable. Export PNG and a browsable local index.
- Capture actual shared-renderer cells and their foreground/background colors,
  with a verified faithful device-style rendering. Clearly label cell-buffer
  renders versus actual console readbacks/screenshots. No AI-created stand-ins.
- Show both normal character size and enlarged detail. Include relevant blue,
  black,water,underground/castle and snow/monochrome scene backgrounds;inspect
  contrast against terrain and neighboring objects,not only isolated swatches.
- Include facing/inversion,animation,color phases,partial emergence/clipping,
  connected objects and overlap where those change visibility. Explain genuine
  aliases rather than silently dropping states with similar pictures.
- Maintain an owner-review checklist linking each case to its image,verdict,
  requested change,receiving repair cohort and final accepted result. Completion
  requires every manifest row accounted for;unreviewed rows remain visible.
- Use isolated probes or direct buffer rendering without taking user focus,
  injecting global input or disrupting the active main task.

without committing protected derived products. Verify shape/color expectations,
