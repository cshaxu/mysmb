# Source And Research Policy

Owner-supplied Nintendo ROMs are local research/build inputs only. Never commit, publish, attach, fixture, default-load, or distribute ROM bytes, CHR, audio, screenshots containing recoverable protected assets, generated C/data, or an executable embedding them.

Before any ROM, disassembly, translation, tool, or third-party source is used, the active packet records provenance, license/redistributability status, exact purpose, local containment, and verification. Existing public translations are research references until separately reviewed; they are never copied or transliterated by default.

Raw traces are ignored, bounded, and deleted after their admitted diagnostic use. Tracked evidence contains only neutral metadata, addresses, hashes where lawful, conclusions, and reproducible commands without machine-local paths.
